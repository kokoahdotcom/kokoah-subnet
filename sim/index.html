Subnet Incentive Simulator

Design v5.3: the day's miner pool is shared by fee credit times a multiplier between the floor and 1.0 (skill and holding), each miner's pay is capped at the value of the Kokoah fee they paid times their multiplier, and the rest of the pool is burned. Fees buy tokens back on the owner's cadence. Change any dial or any miner and everything recomputes. Volume means the notional of fills per day; each fill pays the builder fee once.

Net cost = fees to Hyperliquid + fees to Kokoah + hidden-leg fees − value of the tokens received at the day's price. For a cheat (no real market bet) a negative net cost means the cheat profits. Hidden leg bps: the other venue's fee per side on the same notional, for hedgers (negative for a maker rebate). Skill g is the 0–1 score from the design (confidence × quality, no drawdown): a coin-flip account averages about 0.07, a steady grinder 0.3, a real edge 0.35, a strong one 0.56. Holds % is the share of paid tokens the miner keeps staked (the holding factor); the rest is sold into the pool every day. HL fee × is this account's Hyperliquid fee as a share of the base tier (1 = tier 0; 0.32 = tier 6 with the 40% Diamond staking discount): the second cap follows it.

How the day is computed

  1. 7,200 new tokens a day; the miner slot is the pool. Fee credit = Kokoah fees paid, taker fills at 1.0, maker fills at the maker credit.
  2. Multiplier = floor + skill weight × min(1, g ÷ top skill) + holding weight × holds. Share = pool × (credit × multiplier) ÷ the sum over miners.
  3. Cap (in dollars) = multiplier × the smaller of (pay cap × fee credit) and (second cap × (Hyperliquid fee + Kokoah fee) on the Kokoah leg); divided by the token price for tokens. Pay = the smaller of share and cap. The pool above the sum of pays is burned.
  4. Miners sell (1 − holds) of their pay into the pool every day (constant product). Every N days the accumulated fees buy tokens from the pool and burn them; staker buying is daily.
  5. Paid ÷ revenue never exceeds the pay cap, so the fees always cover what miners could sell.
