Subnet Incentive Simulator

Day-by-day model of the trader subnet: miners trade through the builder code, the pool of new tokens is split by fees and by size × skill × holding, miners sell a share, the builder fees buy tokens back. Change any dial or any miner and everything recomputes. Volume means the notional of fills per day; each fill pays the builder fee once.

Net cost = fees to Hyperliquid + fees to Kokoah + hidden-leg fees − value of the tokens received at the day's price. For a cheat (no real market bet) a negative net cost means the cheat profits. Hidden leg bps: the other venue's fee per side on the same notional, for hedgers (negative for a maker rebate). Skill g is the 0–1 score from the design: a coin-flip account averages 0.18, a decent trader about 0.6, a strong one 0.8.

How the day is computed

  1. 7,200 new tokens a day: the miner slot goes to miners by weight; the owner slot is locked; validators and stakers sell their set share.
  2. Fee credit = Kokoah fees paid, taker fills at 1.0, maker fills at the maker credit. Skill score Q = (30 × volume + λ × 30 × profit, if positive) × g × holding multiplier (0.5 + 0.5 × kept share).
  3. Weight = fee half × credit share + the rest × Q share. If nobody has skill, the whole pool goes by fee credit.
  4. Miners sell their set share of the tokens into the pool (constant-product, price = TAO reserve ÷ token reserve). Validators' sales join them.
  5. Kokoah buys tokens back with the buyback share of the day's fees, plus staker net buying; bought tokens are burned.
  6. "Price the buyback holds" = (buyback + staker buying) ÷ tokens sold per day, in TAO: where the price settles with no other demand.
