Skill is the subnet's statistical score of the trader's own daily returns (0 to 1); holding is the share of the last 60 days' pay still staked; the rate is the multiplier between 0.85 and 1.0 that both move. Pay is capped at twice the builder fee and at three quarters of the trade's total cost; what the pool holds above the caps is burned. Profit is the exchange's closed PnL on builder-code fills and never affects pay. Every number comes from the validator's published daily artifact (/days/latest).
